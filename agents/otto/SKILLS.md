# SKILLS: Otto — Playbooks

## 1. Keyword strategy for the brand's niche
Start from the target keywords in the brief's Search & social section; if none are given, derive them from the products & services in Overview crossed with the Locations & service areas.
1. Split the universe into intents:
   - **Commercial (money pages)**: "[product/service] [town]" for each service area, "[category] [region]", "[product] near me", "[secondary service] [area]".
   - **Comparison/consideration**: "[category] vs [cheaper alternative]", "[named competitor] alternatives" (for each competitor in the brief), "best [category] companies [region]", "is [category] worth it".
   - **Informational (cluster fuel)**: "how much does [product] cost [region]", "[product] ideas", "[option A] vs [option B]", the questions customers ask before buying.
2. Prioritise by (a) buying intent, (b) service-area relevance, (c) realistic difficulty — a specialist business beats national chains on local + quality modifiers, not on the bare category head term.
3. Validate by searching the live web for each priority term: log who ranks, SERP features (local pack, AI Overview present?), and content format Google rewards. Label all tool volumes as indicative.
4. Map every keyword to exactly one target URL; resolve cannibalisation before creating new pages.

## 2. Blog content engine — clusters
Run four evergreen clusters, each with a pillar page and 6–10 supporting posts interlinked. Derive the topics from the products & services in the brief's Overview:
1. **How-to / planning guides**: how to choose, plan, size, prepare for or get the most from the product or service.
2. **Materials, methods & craftsmanship**: what it's made of, how it's made or delivered, the choices between options, what sets the brand's approach apart (from Positioning & USPs).
3. **Cost & buying guides**: "[Product] cost guide [region] [year]" (the highest-value GEO asset — include real price bands scaled to the brief), finance options explained if offered (compliance-checked), brand vs cheaper-alternative comparison, what affects price.
4. **Trends & inspiration**: annual trends piece, seasonal pieces tied to the brief's Seasonality, case studies of real work ("A [product] project in [service-area town]") — case studies double as local SEO and social proof.
Each brief specifies: target keyword + secondary terms, searcher scenario, required expert input (quote a named person from the business), internal links (to the conversion page + sibling posts), FAQ block, and one citable statistic or table.
Cadence: 2–4 high-quality pieces monthly; update the cost guide and trends pieces every 6–12 months (refresh dates honestly).

## 3. On-page SEO checklist (apply to every key page)
- Title tag ≤60 chars, keyword near front, brand at end; unique meta description ≤155 chars with a reason to click (the CTA from the brief's Goals).
- One H1; logical H2/H3s that mirror sub-questions; FAQ section with real questions.
- Primary keyword in H1, first 100 words, one H2, and image alt text — naturally.
- Internal links: every post links to a money page and 2–3 cluster siblings with descriptive anchors; money pages link to the contact page and to any location page.
- Images: real photography of the brand's work, compressed (WebP), descriptive filenames and alts ("[product]-[descriptor]-[town].webp").
- Schema: Organization + LocalBusiness sitewide (one per location in the brief); FAQPage on guides; Product/Service where apt; BreadcrumbList.
- Clear CTA above the fold and at end, following the brief's Goals.
- E-E-A-T: named author from the business, bio, last-updated date, real imagery.

## 4. Backlink playbooks
1. **Digital PR for the niche**: create linkable assets — an annual trends report for the category, a cost-of-[product] data piece, a "most popular [choice]" survey. Pitch to the trade and lifestyle press that covers the brief's category, and to local titles across the brief's service areas.
2. **Journalist requests**: monitor #journorequest, Qwoted/ResponseSource-style services and live-search for journalists writing features in the niche; respond with expert commentary from the business within hours, with a headshot and credentials.
3. **Supplier & partner links**: request "stockist/partner/approved installer" listings from the brands the business sells, uses or is accredited by, and from trade bodies. Easy, relevant, white-hat.
4. **Directories**: complete profiles on the directories that matter for the brief's category (Houzz if it's a home or design business, the sector's equivalents otherwise), plus general and local business directories and chambers — consistent NAP, real photos, link to the matching location page. These double as GEO entity signals.
5. **Local sponsorships/community**: local events, schools, clubs across the brief's service areas — genuine local links beat generic ones for local SEO.
Never buy links or join link schemes. Track referring domains monthly.

## 5. Local landing page template ("[product/service] [area]")
Build one per service area listed in the brief, each with genuinely local substance:
1. H1: "[Product/Service] in [Area]" + intro referencing local context (the kind of properties, people or circumstances typical of the area that shape the job).
2. 1–2 real case studies from that area or nearby, with photos.
3. Location block: the nearest location from the brief's Locations & service areas if the business has premises — address, map embed, directions/drive time from [Area], link to its GBP; if the business is service-only, a clear statement of coverage and response times for [Area].
4. Services summary, team introduction, review snippets (with permission), area-specific FAQ (3–5 questions), LocalBusiness/FAQ schema, CTA from the brief's Goals.
5. No page ships if it's only a find-and-replace of another — add or wait for local proof.

## 6. Technical SEO basics (monthly check)
- Crawl for broken links, redirect chains, duplicate titles/meta; fix or ticket.
- Core Web Vitals and mobile usability (image-heavy portfolio or product pages are the usual culprits — lazy-load, compress).
- XML sitemap current and submitted; robots.txt sane; one canonical version of every URL (https, single host).
- Search Console: coverage errors, manual actions, security issues — escalate immediately if found.
- Structured data validation after any template change.

## 7. GEO — Generative Engine Optimization (AI search visibility)
Goal: when someone asks ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity/AI Overviews "best [category] companies near [service-area town]" or "how much does [product] cost [region]", the brand is named or cited.
1. **Citable content**: AI engines quote pages with direct answers, named statistics, tables and clear price bands. Lead the cost guide with a one-paragraph answer ("In [region], [product] typically costs [low]–[high] as of [year]…", scaled to the brief's price band) then detail.
2. **FAQ schema everywhere relevant**: self-contained Q&As (40–80 word answers) that can be lifted verbatim.
3. **Entity consistency**: identical brand name, addresses, phone, founding facts and descriptions (all from the brief) across site, GBP, directories, social bios, Wikipedia-adjacent sources. Inconsistency erodes AI confidence.
4. **llms.txt**: maintain /llms.txt summarising the company, services, locations, key pages and canonical facts in clean markdown for AI crawlers; keep it current with the brief's offers and locations.
5. **Allow AI crawlers** (GPTBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot) in robots.txt unless instructed otherwise — being uncrawlable means being uncited.
6. **Third-party presence**: AI answers lean on "best of" listicles, review platforms and forums. Pursue inclusion in credible "best [category] [region]" round-ups; maintain strong review profiles on Google and the sector's review platforms; monitor Reddit/forum mentions and (transparently, no astroturfing) ensure accurate information exists.
7. **Prompt tracking**: monthly, run a fixed set of 10–15 prompts through live web/AI search checks (built from the brief's products, service areas and competitors) and log whether the brand appears, who does, and which sources are cited — then target those sources.

## 8. Measurement & reporting
- Stack: GA4 (consent-mode compliant), Search Console, rank tracking for priority terms, referring-domain log, GEO prompt log.
- Monthly pack: organic leads/conversions on the brief's Goals with trend; priority rankings movement; top growing/declining queries; content shipped and early performance; links earned; technical issues; AI-citation scorecard; next month's top 3 priorities.
- Attribute honestly: for considered purchases at the brief's price band, organic assists on long journeys mean last-click understates value — report assisted conversions and say so.
- Every number gets a source and date; estimates are labelled indicative, always.
