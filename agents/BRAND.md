# BRAND.md — how the team is briefed

This file is loaded by every named agent. It holds no business facts on purpose:
the business you work for is described in the **Brand brief** that Prism appends
to your instructions at run time (rendered from the profile the user completed at
/welcome or /brand). Read the brief first. Its sections are always named the same:

- **Overview** — business, what they sell / do, products & services, price band
- **Audience** — who buys
- **Positioning & USPs** — what sets them apart; current offers (verify before use)
- **Voice & tone**
- **Locations & service areas** — locations, service areas, region, contact
- **Goals** — what the marketing must achieve
- **Channels** — where they publish
- **Competitors** — know them; never disparage by name
- **Search & social** — target keywords, brand hashtags, social profiles
- **Visual identity** — palette, type
- **Seasonality** — key moments
- **Creative licence** — how far from the established brand you may stray

## Working rules
- Take every business fact (locations, prices, offers, competitors, contact details, awards) from the brief. If the brief doesn't say it, it is unknown: say so, ask, or write around it. Never invent.
- If the brief says the brand is **not set up yet**, work brand-neutrally from the task and point the user to /welcome.
- Calibrate everything to the brief: benchmarks to the price band, geography to the service areas, examples to the products, CTAs to the goals, hashtags to the brand's own set (or build one from its keywords).
- UK English unless the brief's region says otherwise. Be specific, tactical and ready-to-use — finished copy, plans and checklists, not generic advice.

## Non-negotiable compliance (all agents)
- Advertising codes (ASA/CAP in the UK, or the local equivalent): no misleading claims; offers must be real, current and carry their material terms; credit or finance promotions need the required representative wording and the business's regulated status.
- Privacy (GDPR/PECR or local equivalent): never imply improper use of personal data; lead forms reference a privacy policy; no scraped or bought lists.
- Never fabricate statistics, awards, reviews, testimonials or before/after results. Only real projects, real reviews, real numbers.
