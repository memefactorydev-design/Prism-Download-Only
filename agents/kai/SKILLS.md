# SKILLS.md — Kai's TikTok Playbooks

## 1. Profile & TikTok SEO
1. Bio formula: what + where + CTA in three lines — "[what the brief's Overview says they do] | [locations or service area from the brief] | [CTA from the Goals] ↓". Lead keyword in the display name ("[Brand] · [primary product/service]") — TikTok search reads it.
2. TikTok is a search engine: target one query per search-led video ("how much does [product] cost", "[product] ideas", "[option A] vs [option B]") — queries come from the brief's Search & social section or are derived from its products + service areas. Put the keyword in the on-screen text, say it aloud in the first 5 seconds, and repeat it in the caption's first sentence.
3. Check Analytics → Traffic sources → Search monthly; the terms people found you by become next month's videos.
4. Use playlists to build binge-paths: "Project/Product Showcases", "[Product] Costs Explained", "Behind the Scenes", "Tips & How-To". Playlists boost session time and signal topical authority.
5. Link-in-bio to the conversion page named in the brief's Goals, UTM-tagged (business accounts get the link at 1k followers; until then, drive "visit our website" verbally and via profile).

## 2. Formats That Perform
1. **Cost breakdown** (the search banker): "[Real total] — where every pound went" over b-roll with on-screen line items. Few businesses in any considered-purchase niche do this honestly; it prints authority and search traffic. 45–90 seconds. Real, approved figures only, scaled to the brief's price band.
2. **Before/after:** 1-second hold on the worst before angle, beat-matched cut or reveal wipe to the after, slow pan, end card. 15–25 seconds — the shortest format; let it loop cleanly for rewatch rate. Works for any product or service with a visible outcome.
3. **Process/craft ASMR:** the making, the preparation, the fitting, the finishing — whatever the business's hands actually do. Low or original audio, minimal text. This is the moat: competitors who outsource or resell cannot film it. At least one weekly.
4. **POV walk-through:** phone-gimbal walk through a finished job, a location from the brief or the product in use, with voiceover telling brief → decisions → result. 60–120 seconds; watch time is the top ranking signal and a story arc holds it.
5. **"Things that just make sense" / details list:** trending list format over detail shots of the product or service. Adapts endlessly, always on-brand.
6. **Comment-reply video:** answer a real comment (use the sticker). Builds community and mines infinite topics.
7. **Day-in-the-life / on-the-job:** a delivery, install, service or production day with timestamps. Humanises the brand; strong follow-driver.
8. **Photo-mode carousel:** 6–10 stills of one job or product with swipe-worthy captions; TikTok pushes photo posts hard and they're cheap to produce from existing shoots.
9. Series > one-offs: number them ("[Product] Costs Explained — Part 4"). Numbered series train follows and give the algorithm a binge signal.

## 3. Hooks (the first second decides everything)
1. Frame one must be the most arresting frame you have — never a logo, fade or establishing shot. Text hook of 5–9 words on top of it.
2. Proven hook patterns: cost transparency ("[Real price]. Here's where it went."), time contrast ("[N] years with the before. [N] weeks to fix it."), mistake-framing ("The [product] mistake in every plan"), insider knowledge ("What [the brief's experts] notice first"), specificity ("A [product] for a family who [specific habit]"), payoff promise ("Wait for the [feature]" — only when the feature delivers).
3. Log every video's hook + 3-second hold rate in a hook library; reuse the top decile structures with new jobs.
4. Re-hook long videos every 15–20 seconds with a mini-payoff or text change — watch-through is cumulative.

## 4. Trends, Sounds & Timing
1. Ride a sound in its rising window (24–72 hours; check if the sound's top videos are <3 days old). After a week, it's wallpaper.
2. Filter every trend through the brand floor: would this play in front of the brand's best customers? Adapt the *structure* of a trend (list format, reveal timing) even when the audio doesn't fit — structure carries the reach, audio is optional.
3. Post 3–5×/week; consistency beats bursts. Peak windows to test for the brief's region: 7–9am, 12–1pm, 7–10pm local time; let 90 days of analytics override defaults.
4. Delete nothing; old videos resurface via search for years. Update with a comment ("[current year] pricing in part 2") instead.
5. Use CapCut natively (TikTok-owned, no watermark penalty) with saved brand templates built from the brief's Visual identity: consistent font, lower-third style, end card.

## 5. Community, DMs & Lead Capture
1. Reply to every comment in the first 2 hours after posting — early engagement velocity feeds distribution. Pin the smartest question.
2. "How much?" comments get a warm, honest range from the brief's price band plus an invitation: "Depends on [the main variables] — this one was around [real figure]. Happy to talk through yours: [the brief's conversion action], link in bio."
3. Weekly comment-reply video from the best question (see formats). Recurring questions go to Otto for the blog and to the FAQ highlight.
4. DM enquiries: respond within 4 working hours with the booking/quote link or an invitation to a location if the brief lists one; log every lead with its source video so the monthly report ties content to revenue.
5. Go Live monthly — from a location in the brief if there is one, otherwise from the workshop, the floor or the job — once past 1k followers. Lives boost overall account distribution and let warm followers ask questions in real time.

## 6. Creator Collabs & Spark Ads
1. Target creators (10k–200k, engaged audiences in the brief's region) whose followers match the brief's Audience section for visits, product trials and "[doing X] with…" series. Gift products/services or pay fees, always labelled #ad with the branded-content toggle.
2. Whitelist winning organic videos as **Spark Ads** (with Scout's research on audiences/competitors first) — native-format ads with the organic video's social proof outperform studio-made ad creative on TikTok.
3. Duet/stitch selectively: respond to viral fails or questions in the niche with the expert take — borrows the original's reach for authority-building.
4. Benchmark quarterly: what are the brief's named competitors (or the top three in the niche if none are listed) and the biggest global accounts in the category doing on TikTok, and where is the gap this brand can own? (Usually: honest pricing + a genuine process nobody else shows.)
